================================================================================
00b_tuba_transient_valve_detector — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A specialized Tuba Tongue-Attack ("tah" / "dah" / slap-tongue) and Mechanical Valve-Click / Air-Chuff Transient Detector designed to drive dubstep synth envelopes and freeze pitch trackers during messy note transitions.
Uses a dual-band Teager-Kaiser Energy Operator (TKEO: psi(x[n]) = x[n-1]^2 - x[n]*x[n-2], which responds in 3 samples to instantaneous changes in amplitude and frequency) paired with fast/slow differential spectral flux followers across two bands:
- Tongue Wavefront Band (260 Hz SVF bandpass): Captures brass lip/tongue attacks.
- Valve-Click & Chuff Band (2.2 kHz 2-stage SVF highpass): Captures piston/rotary valve clacks and unpitched breath bursts.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo (or Mono on in1) Tuba Audio Input
- in3: Optional Pitch Confidence [0.0 to 1.0] (from out2 of 00_tuba_sift_yin_tracker; automatically holds the Freeze Gate high if periodicity drops below 0.45 during a valve slur)
- out1: Master Tongue / Attack Trigger Pulse (2.5 ms impulse at 1.0 on every attack)
- out2: Velocity-Sensitive Dubstep Synth Mod Envelope [0.0 to 1.0] (patch to filter cutoff, FM index, comb sweep, or allpass dispersion!)
- out3: Note-Transition Pitch Freeze Gate [0 or 1] (stays at 1.0 for freeze_hold_ms during valve clicks and unpitched attack chaos so downstream pitch effects never glitch)
- out4: Isolated Valve-Click Trigger Pulse (2.5 ms impulse at 1.0 triggered specifically when high-frequency mechanical valve clack dominates)

PARAMETERS (4)

1. sensitivity
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Default: 0.65)
   - Taper: Exponential threshold curve (3.2 * 0.11^(x^1.2)) with a 28 ms refractory debounce window to prevent double-triggering on low pedal-note waveform peaks.
   - Function: Controls the onset-flux ratio required to fire a transient trigger. Low values respond only to hard fortissimo slap-tongues; high values catch delicate legato tongue articulations and light valve clicks.

2. env_decay_ms
   - Range: 15.0 to 1500.0 ms (Default: 220.0 ms)
   - Taper: Direct decay duration in milliseconds.
   - Function: Sets the decay time of the velocity-sensitive dubstep modulation envelope on out2. Short settings (30–90 ms) create percussive tearout transient whips; longer settings (200–800 ms) create full breath-triggered dubstep filter drops.

3. freeze_hold_ms
   - Range: 5.0 to 150.0 ms (Default: 35.0 ms)
   - Taper: Direct hold duration in milliseconds.
   - Function: Sets how long the Transition Freeze Gate (out3) stays high (1.0) after a tongue or valve transient, bridging over the 15–40 ms chaotic window before the brass bore locks into its new standing-wave regime.

4. env_curve
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Default: -0.35)
   - Taper: Exponential power exponent (3^(-env_curve), ranging from cubically concave 1/3 power to cubically convex 3.0 power).
   - Function: Shapes the curvature of the out2 modulation envelope:
     * -1.0 to -0.3: Snappy, fast-dropping riddim whip contour
     *  0.0: Linear decay ramp
     * +0.3 to +1.0: Rounded, hanging sustain swell that drops off at the end
