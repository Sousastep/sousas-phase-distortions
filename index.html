RNBO codebox~ Tuba Effects
Parameter Reference

◆
Project

Twenty-two stereo codebox~ processors for performing live dubstep and riddim on an amplified tuba: pitch/phase trackers and analyzers, twelve single-purpose phase-dispersion / phase-distortion / FM / comb effects, and six playable instruments.

◇
Purpose

A single-page parameter reference: for every effect, its inlets and outlets and each parameter’s range, taper, and musical function in conventional units (Hz, ms, dB).

→
Signal Chain

Tuba (mic/DI) → trackers & analyzers (00–00d) → one or more of 01–12 → optional wobble filter → out  |  or a single instrument (13–18) end-to-end. Pitch arrives on in3 (from fluid.pitch~ or 00_tuba_freq_tracker); a 1-bar audio-rate phasor arrives on in3/in4 where a rhythm is used.

About This Reference #

Each effect below is presented with its overview, its inlets & outlets, and a table of its parameters. Within each parameter table you get:

  • Range — the full span in conventional musical units, including the default where the source file specifies one.
  • Taper — how the control value maps to the underlying DSP quantity (linear, exponential, power curve, dB-to-linear, etc.), so you know where the knob’s resolution is concentrated.
  • Function — what the parameter actually does to the sound or to the signal flow. Multi-zone controls list their zones.

Conventions used throughout:

  • f0 is the tracked tuba fundamental in Hz; fs is the sample rate.
  • Effects without a pitch inlet are 10 (its in3 is the 1-bar phasor) and 12. In 02, 05, 08, and 11 the pitch inlet is optional — it only tunes an internal detail when connected.
  • Effects with a rhythmic section take a 1-bar phasor and fall back to an internal ~0.5 Hz LFO when it is unpatched.
  • All parameters are smoothed/slewed internally; sweep-heavy controls quote the slew time (typically 5 ms, 12 ms for waveguide pitch).

Routing Map #

Two rules cover almost every patch:

  1. Audio: every effect takes stereo audio on in1/in2 and returns stereo audio on out1/out2, so any effect can feed any other. The exceptions worth naming are 01 harmonic_isolator, which is the preferred audio source for 04, 05, and 12, and the instruments 13–18, which take the raw tuba directly.
  2. Pitch: every pitch-aware effect reads tracked f0 on in3, normally from out1 of 00 tuba_freq_tracker (or fluid.pitch~). Effects without a pitch inlet are 10 and 12; in 02, 05, 08, and 11 the inlet is optional.

Trackers, Analyzers & Analysis Front-End #

These four processors sit ahead of the effects. They turn the tuba into machine-readable control signals — tracked fundamental, confidence, phase-locked phasor, transient triggers, per-harmonic amplitude and instantaneous frequency — and 00d can be patched 1-to-1 from 00c to resynthesize from them.

00 tuba_freq_tracker 4 parameters #

A custom Tuba-Specialized Hybrid Pitch & Phasor Tracker that outperforms generic FFT-based YinFFT (fluid.pitch~) for live low-brass dubstep performance by uniting three complementary DSP architectures inside a single RNBO codebox~:

  1. 4x-Decimated Recursive Sliding YIN (CMNDF): Computes the Cumulative Mean Normalized Difference Function continuously in the time domain with parabolic sub-sample interpolation and a 3-point transient median filter, updating every 32 samples (0.66 ms) instead of waiting for a 2048/4096-sample FFT frame.
  2. Instantaneous Frequency Tracking (IFT): Isolates the tuba’s fundamental via a dual-stage adaptive SVF bandpass filter tracked to the YIN anchor, computes the analytic signal sa(t) = A(t)·ej·θ(t) via a 4-stage quadrature Hilbert transform, and differentiates the unwrapped phase dθ/dt on every sample (zero window latency).
  3. Phase-Locked Loop (PLL) Fusion: Uses YIN as an unbreakable octave/harmonic anchor while using IFT to track fast lip bends, vibrato, and glissandi within a ±18% window, holding the last valid pitch cleanly on note releases.
Inlets & Outlets
  • in1 / in2 — Stereo (or Mono on in1) Tuba Audio Input
  • in3 — Optional External Pitch Hint in Hz (if fluid.pitch~ is patched here, blends with the internal YIN anchor for redundant lock)
  • out1 — Tracked Fundamental Pitch f0 in Hz (patch directly into in3 of effects 01–18!)
  • out2 — Pitch Confidence / Periodicity [0.0 to 1.0]
  • out3 — Phase-Locked Tuba Phasor [0.0 to 1.0) (phase-locked sawtooth ramp at f0)
  • out4 — Pure Phase-Locked Fundamental Sine Wave [−1.0 to 1.0] (scaled by confidence)
Signal Routing
  • inin1 / in2 ← tuba mic/DI; in3 ← an optional external pitch hint from fluid.pitch~ or another tracker’s out1. This is the head of the chain — nothing upstream.
  • outout1 (tracked f0) → in3 of 00c, 01, 03, 04, 06, 07, 09, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, and 18; optionally in3 of 02, 05, 08, and 11.
  • outout2 (pitch confidence) → in3 of 00b tuba_transient_valve_detector.
  • outout4 (phase-locked sine) → any carrier/modulator inlet, as an alternative to 01 out3 / out4.

00b tuba_transient_valve_detector 4 parameters #

A specialized Tuba Tongue-Attack (“tah” / “dah” / slap-tongue) and Mechanical Valve-Click / Air-Chuff Transient Detector designed to drive dubstep synth envelopes and freeze pitch trackers during messy note transitions.

Uses a dual-band Teager-Kaiser Energy Operator (TKEO: ψ(x[n]) = x[n−1]² − x[n]·x[n−2], which responds in 3 samples to instantaneous changes in amplitude and frequency) paired with fast/slow differential spectral flux followers across two bands:

  • Tongue Wavefront Band (260 Hz SVF bandpass): Captures brass lip/tongue attacks.
  • Valve-Click & Chuff Band (2.2 kHz 2-stage SVF highpass): Captures piston/rotary valve clacks and unpitched breath bursts.
Inlets & Outlets
  • in1 / in2 — Stereo (or Mono on in1) Tuba Audio Input
  • in3 — Optional Pitch Confidence [0.0 to 1.0] (from out2 of 00_tuba_freq_tracker; automatically holds the Freeze Gate high if periodicity drops below 0.45 during a valve slur)
  • out1 — Master Tongue / Attack Trigger Pulse (2.5 ms impulse at 1.0 on every attack)
  • out2 — Velocity-Sensitive Dubstep Synth Mod Envelope [0.0 to 1.0] (patch to filter cutoff, FM index, comb sweep, or allpass dispersion!)
  • out3 — Note-Transition Pitch Freeze Gate [0 or 1] (stays at 1.0 for freeze_hold_ms during valve clicks and unpitched attack chaos so downstream pitch effects never glitch)
  • out4 — Isolated Valve-Click Trigger Pulse (2.5 ms impulse at 1.0 triggered specifically when high-frequency mechanical valve clack dominates)
Signal Routing
  • inin3 ← out2 of 00 tuba_freq_tracker (pitch confidence; holds the Freeze Gate high when periodicity drops below 0.45 during a valve slur). Audio on in1 / in2.
  • outout3 (freeze gate) → in4 of 00c tuba_4harmonic_IFT_analyzer.
  • outout2 (mod envelope) → filter cutoff, FM index, comb sweep, or allpass dispersion; out1 / out4 (trigger pulses) → any envelope, gate, or sample-and-hold in the host patch.

00c tuba_4harmonic_IFT_analyzer 4 parameters #

A 4-Harmonic Instantaneous Frequency & Amplitude (IFT) Analyzer for the live tuba. Simultaneously locks four dual-stage high-Q SVF bandpass filters onto the first four partials of the tuba (f1 = f0, f2 = 2·f0, f3 = 3·f0, f4 = 4·f0), passes each isolated partial through its own 4-stage quadrature IIR Hilbert transform, and differentiates the unwrapped phase dθk/dt on every sample.

Captures both the exact per-harmonic amplitude envelope A1..A4 (the tuba’s dynamic spectral roll-off / brassiness) and the exact per-harmonic instantaneous frequency f1..f4 (revealing nonlinear bore wave-steepening, ghost-note inharmonicity, and micro-detuning during attacks). Also outputs the real-time Harmonic Spectral Centroid [1.0 to 4.0] on out9 so your embouchure brightness (soft vs. brassy cuivré) can directly drive dubstep filter cutoffs or FM indices!

Inlets & Outlets
  • in1 / in2 — Stereo (or Mono on in1) Tuba Audio Input
  • in3 — Tracked Fundamental Pitch f0 in Hz (from out1 of 00_tuba_freq_tracker or fluid.pitch~; automatically falls back to an internal zero-crossing tracker if <= 0)
  • in4 — Optional Freeze Gate [0 or 1] (from out3 of 00b_tuba_transient_valve_detector; freezes all 4 frequencies and the spectral centroid during valve transitions)
  • out1..out4 — Instantaneous Frequencies f1, f2, f3, f4 in Hz
  • out5..out8 — Instantaneous Amplitudes A1, A2, A3, A4
  • out9 — Real-Time Brass Spectral Centroid Ratio [1.0 to 4.0] (weighted mean harmonic number: 1.0 = pure fundamental, 2.5+ = bright fortissimo brass)
Signal Routing
  • inin3 ← out1 of 00 tuba_freq_tracker (or fluid.pitch~, with an internal zero-crossing fallback when ≤ 0). in4 ← out3 of 00b tuba_transient_valve_detector.
  • outout1..out4, out5..out8, and out9 → in1..in4, in5..in8, and in9 of 00d tuba_4op_modfm_resynthesizer — a direct 1-to-1 patch.
  • outout9 (spectral centroid) → any cutoff or FM index that should follow brassiness rather than pitch.
Parameters (4)

Controls the bandwidth of the 4 harmonic isolation filters before the Hilbert IFT stages. At 1.0, out5..out8 always sum to 1.0 regardless of how softly or loudly you play, giving you pure timbre/formant weights for spectral-envelope transfer (Lazzarini & Timoney ModFM vocoder, Approach 6).

00d tuba_4op_modfm_resynthesizer 8 parameters #

A 4-Operator Adaptive ModFM & Phaseshaper Vocoder Resynthesizer designed to be patched directly 1-to-1 from the 9 outlets of 00c_tuba_4harmonic_IFT_analyzer.txt (plus an optional 1-bar phasor on in10).

Implements the ModFM Synthesis Vocoder concept from Lazzarini & Timoney (ICMC 2011 / DAFx-08) combined with SMC 2010 PolyBLEP Hard-Sync Phaseshaping:

  • Each of the 4 tracked tuba harmonics (f1..f4, A1..A4) drives its own ModFM operator sk(t) = Ak(t) · carrierk(φk) · exp(k_op · (cos(2π·φ_mod) − 1)), generating strictly monotonic Modified Bessel sidebands around each partial that never cancel or clip.
  • The tuba’s real-time Spectral Centroid (in9) automatically couples your acoustic brass brightness (soft dark tone vs. brassy fortissimo) to the ModFM index and sync formant ratio.
  • Measures the real-time inharmonic deviation of partials 2, 3, and 4 from integer multiples of f1 and lets you scale it from pure harmonic lock (0.0) up to 4x exaggerated ghost-note metallic clangor.
Inlets & Outlets
  • in1..in4 — Harmonics 1..4 Instantaneous Frequencies f1, f2, f3, f4 in Hz (from out1..out4 of 00c)
  • in5..in8 — Harmonics 1..4 Instantaneous Amplitudes A1, A2, A3, A4 (from out5..out8 of 00c)
  • in9 — Real-Time Brass Spectral Centroid Ratio [1.0 to 4.0] (from out9 of 00c)
  • in10 — Optional 1-Bar Audio-Rate Phasor [0.0 to 1.0] (syncs rhythmic ModFM/Sync wobble; falls back to internal 0.5 Hz LFO if unpatched)
  • out1 / out2 — Stereo 4-Operator ModFM Resynthesizer Output
Signal Routing
  • inin1..in9 ← out1..out9 of 00c tuba_4harmonic_IFT_analyzer (1-to-1). in10 ← host/sequencer 1-bar phasor, with an internal 0.5 Hz LFO fallback.
  • outout1 / out2 ends the analysis branch — it is a resynthesized audio output, not a modulation source.

Single-Purpose Effects (01–12) #

Twelve effects in the Unix spirit: each does one thing well, each takes at most 4 parameters, and they are designed to be chained — 01 in particular is the recommended front end for 04, 05, and 16, while 09, 10, and 14 all share a waveguide comb core.

01 harmonic_isolator 4 parameters #

Isolates the fundamental or a chosen overtone of the live tuba using a pitch-tracked RBJ peaking filter (+0 to +48 dB) cascaded into a dual-stage state-variable bandpass filter. Decouples breath dynamics via fast envelope normalization so the tuba approximates a pure, phase-locked sine wave ready to act as a carrier or modulator in downstream FM, phase distortion, or waveshaping effects.

Inlets & Outlets
  • in1 / in2 — Stereo Audio Input (Left / Right)
  • in3 — Tracked Fundamental Pitch in Hz (from fluid.pitch~; automatically falls back to internal zero-crossing tracker if <= 0)
  • out1 / out2 — Stereo Filtered & Boosted Output
  • out3 / out4 — Pure Normalized Unit-Sine Output (for patching into downstream modulation inlets)
Signal Routing
  • inin3 ← out1 of 00 tuba_freq_tracker (or fluid.pitch~, with an internal zero-crossing fallback). Audio on in1 / in2.
  • outout1 / out2 → audio in1 / in2 of 04 hilbert_phasor_sync, 05 phaseshaper_supersaw_tri, and 12 adaa_chebyshev_wavefolder, each of which names 01 as its preferred source — or of any other effect in the set.
  • outout3 / out4 (unit sine) → downstream carrier/modulator inlets of FM, phase-distortion, and waveshaping stages.

02 allpass_disperser 4 parameters #

A pure feed-forward cascade of up to 32 second-order biquad allpass filters per channel inspired by Kilohearts Disperser (“Disburser”). Because allpass filters have a flat magnitude response (0 dB across all frequencies) but a peaked group-delay curve around their corner frequency, stacking 32 stages multiplies the phase dispersion without carving phaser notches. Brass articulations and distorted harmonic spikes are unfolded in time into squelchy, liquid “chirp” transients.

Inlets & Outlets
  • in1 / in2 — Stereo Audio Input (Left / Right)
  • in3 — Optional Tracked Pitch in Hz (if connected > 20 Hz, scales the dispersion corner frequency relative to 110 Hz so the smear tracks the tuba’s register)
  • out1 / out2 — Stereo Phase-Dispersed Audio Output
Signal Routing
  • inin3 ← optional out1 of 00 tuba_freq_tracker (scales the dispersion corner frequency with the register when > 20 Hz). Audio on in1 / in2.
  • outout1 / out2 → audio in1 / in2 of any downstream effect or instrument.

Controls the total accumulated group delay (τ_total = N · τ_single). Low counts tighten sub punch; 16–32 stages create dramatic laser-like transient chirps.

03 coef_mod_allpass_pd 4 parameters #

Implements the ICASSP 2009 paper by Timoney, Lazzarini, Pekonen, and Välimäki (“Spectrally Rich Phase Distortion Sound Synthesis Using an Allpass Filter”). Modulates the coefficient a(t) in [0, 1] of a first-order allpass filter at the tuba’s fundamental frequency using a piecewise sawtooth phase-deviation function smoothed with a hyperbolic tangent mapping (Eq. 6–9). Unlike wavetable Phase Distortion, this applies true phase distortion directly to the full arbitrary brass signal with a richer high-frequency roll-off and no missing harmonics.

Inlets & Outlets
  • in1 / in2 — Stereo Audio Input (Left / Right)
  • in3 — Tracked Fundamental Pitch in Hz (from fluid.pitch~; falls back to internal zero-crossing tracker if <= 0)
  • out1 / out2 — Stereo Allpass Phase-Distorted Output
